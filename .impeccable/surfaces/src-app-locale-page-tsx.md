# Surface: KSA homepage (/en/, /ar/)

## Scope and mode
Persuade. The bilingual homepage of Lonestar Shipping Co. Ltd (Dammam). The service pages and the privacy page inherit this world.

## Audience, job, action
- **Audience:** oil & gas contractors, importers in KSA, overseas suppliers and agents, Saudi exporters.
- **Job:** decide whether Lonestar can move their shipment into, out of or across Saudi Arabia.
- **Action:** request a quote (the shipment-brief form), or contact Dammam by WhatsApp, phone or email.

## Proof and content
- Real: services, scope and offices from the company profile; memberships (member numbers still to come); Dammam contact.
- The stills are illustrative (AI-generated). There are no client logos, testimonials or volumes, and none may be invented.

## Constraints
- AR/EN parity; English is the default.
- PDPL consent on the form.
- Maps must not show political borders.
- Reduced motion must give a static, complete story.

## Memorable moment
The FASAH stamp landing on the customs frame while the route line reaches the customs stop.

## Unresolved
- Real clips from Flow will replace the stills: a frame sequence, same chapters.
- Official Arabic trade name, CR and VAT numbers, member numbers, form endpoint, domain.

## Direction contract
**THESIS:** The homepage is one consignment's journey, not a brochure. It follows a top drive from a Houston quay to a lit rig in the Eastern Province, and each Saudi capability appears where it happens on that route. It refuses the category default of hero slider, service card grid and stats band.

**OWN-WORLD:**
- **Ground:** night navy (#050b16 to #081226), warming only where the photographs reach sunrise.
- **Colour roles:** signal blue #2f80e0 for actions, sky #4fa8ff for the route line, ice #eaf1fb for text, hull #1b3a66 for 1px rules.
- **Shipping-paper vocabulary, rebuilt for the screen:** a route line with stop nodes, a blue-ink customs stamp, and a quote form laid out as a shipment brief in ruled cells.
- **Type:** Sora display set tight, Manrope body, IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
- **Shapes:** flat surfaces; 4px radius on controls only; photographs full-bleed, never in rounded cards.

**STORY:** The visitor sees Lonestar move critical cargo into Saudi Arabia end to end. They believe it because every stop names the Saudi mechanism: Mawani ports, King Fahd International, FASAH, SABER, and permits for out-of-gauge loads. Then they request a quote or WhatsApp Dammam.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:**
- **Image:** full-bleed S1, the Houston quay at night with the top drive mid-lift.
- **Copy block (start-aligned, lower third):** a Sora H1 "Moving the Kingdom's critical cargo." at clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 5.5rem), a one-line subhead, a signal-blue "Request a quote" button and a quiet "Follow the shipment" link.
- **Top bar:** logo at the start; at the end, the nav, the AR/EN switch and the quote button.
- **Route line:** runs along the bottom edge, Houston → Dammam port → King Fahd Intl → FASAH → rig, with the first node lit.

**Signature interaction:** scroll scrubs the journey. Stills push in slowly and crossfade, the route fills node by node, captions change at each stop, the stamp lands at customs, and the last frame resolves into the call to action.

**FORM:** user-pinned "Houston to the Rig" scroll story. The user chose it in this session from four storylines (see hero/STORYBOARD.md). concept-seed was not run because the user and the group brand pin the structure and the world. Seed key: none (user-pinned).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
