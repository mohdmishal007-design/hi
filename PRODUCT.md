## Platform

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis, exported as static HTML. Proposed by Claude and approved by the user ("yes start"). The domain and host are not decided yet.

## Users

Four audiences, all confirmed as targets:

- **Oil & gas contractors in KSA.** Procurement and logistics teams at drilling, EPC and oilfield-service companies, mostly in the Eastern Province. They move rig equipment, oversized cargo and urgent spares against drilling schedules.
- **Importers in KSA.** Companies bringing goods into the Kingdom that need customs clearance through FASAH, SABER conformity certificates, or an importer of record because they lack a trading licence.
- **Overseas suppliers and partner agents.** Foreign manufacturers and forwarders shipping into Saudi Arabia, often freight-collect, who need a dependable Saudi counterpart.
- **Saudi exporters.** Local companies shipping out of the Kingdom by sea, air or road.

Visitors arrive to judge whether Lonestar can handle a specific shipment in Saudi Arabia, and then to request a quote or contact the Dammam office.

## Product Purpose

This is the Saudi website of Lonestar Shipping Co. Ltd (Dammam), part of the Lonestar Shipping group (HQ Dubai). It wins Saudi B2B logistics enquiries.

Success means qualified quote requests and direct contact (phone, email, WhatsApp) for Saudi shipments, especially customs, SABER, import-on-behalf and oil & gas project cargo.

## Positioning

A forwarder with an actual base in the Kingdom (Dammam) and group offices in six countries (U.A.E., K.S.A., Qatar, U.S.A., Angola, China/Hong Kong). It specialises in oil, gas and out-of-gauge project cargo, and handles Saudi-specific steps itself:
- FASAH customs clearance
- SABER certification
- acting as agent or importer for clients without a Saudi trading licence

The group's Houston office lets it run the U.S.→KSA oilfield-equipment lane end to end.

## Operating Context

- **Saudi working week:** Sunday to Thursday, Arabia Standard Time (UTC+3). WhatsApp is the expected first contact channel.
- **Saudi trade systems:** FASAH (ZATCA's customs single window), SABER (SASO's product and shipment conformity certificates), Mawani ports.
- **Gateways:**
  - Sea: King Abdulaziz Port Dammam, Jubail, Ras Al-Khair, Jeddah Islamic Port
  - Air: King Fahd International (DMM), Riyadh (RUH), Jeddah (JED)
  - Land: GCC and Iraq border crossings
- **Cargo vocabulary:** Incoterms, FCL/LCL, breakbulk, out-of-gauge (OOG), dangerous goods (DG), DNV-certified offshore containers.

**Site constraints:**
- Fully bilingual: Arabic (RTL) and English, each on its own URL. **English is the default** for visitors whose browser states no preference.
- Must comply with Saudi PDPL: a privacy notice, consent on forms, and a stated retention period.
- Maps must show official Saudi boundaries, or avoid political geography entirely.

**Undecided or unverified (do not present as fact until confirmed):**
- years of experience (the profile says 15+, the group site says 20+)

- which border crossings it actually runs

## Product Principles

1. Show the Saudi-specific work (FASAH, SABER, import on behalf, Eastern Province project cargo), not a generic forwarder brochure.
2. Every claim must trace to the profile or to Lonestar. A gap is marked; it is never filled with an invented number.
3. Arabic and English are equals: same content, same quality, each written for its reader.
4. Every page leads to a person. Quote, phone, email and WhatsApp are never more than one step away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA in both reading directions.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences: the scroll story must work without animation.
- Phone numbers and email addresses stay readable left-to-right inside Arabic text.
